08 SUMMARY

Seven takeaways for the deck

  1. Monthly throughput.{{PEAK_MONTH}} is the highest full month at {{PEAK_MONTH_MWH}} MWh.
  2. Weekly rhythm.{{WD_TOP_1}} and {{WD_TOP_2}} average {{WD_TOP_1_KWH}} and {{WD_TOP_2_KWH}} kWh. Saturday averages {{WD_SAT_PCT}}% of Monday–Friday.
  3. Time of use.Fleet discharge peaks at {{TOU_PEAK_DIS_HOUR}} and charging peaks at {{TOU_PEAK_CHG_HOUR}} on weekdays.
  4. Daily cycling.Median fleet EFC is {{EFC_MEDIAN}}/day; the highest day reaches {{EFC_MAX}}.
  5. Efficiency.Unit median round-trip efficiency ranges from {{EFF_MEDIAN_MIN}}% to {{EFF_MEDIAN_MAX}}%.
  6. Charge time.Fleet median equivalent full-charge time is {{CHARGE_FLEET_MEDIAN}}h. The fastest unit is {{CHARGE_FASTEST_UNIT}}.
  7. Unit distribution.Unit daily medians range from {{DIST_MEDIAN_MIN}} to {{DIST_MEDIAN_MAX}} EFC; {{DIST_OUTLIER_UNIT}} has the highest single day at {{DIST_OUTLIER_MAX}} EFC.
