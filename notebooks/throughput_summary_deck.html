08 SUMMARY

Seven takeaways for the deck

  1. Monthly throughput.September is the highest full month at 26.1 MWh.
  2. Weekly rhythm.Tuesday and Wednesday average 778 and 723 kWh. Saturday averages 36% of Monday–Friday.
  3. Time of use.Fleet discharge peaks at 16:00 and charging peaks at 08:00 on weekdays.
  4. Daily cycling.Median fleet EFC is 1.16/day; the highest day reaches 10.80.
  5. Efficiency.Unit median round-trip efficiency ranges from 78.9% to 80.4%.
  6. Charge time.Fleet median equivalent full-charge time is 5.17h. The fastest unit is 0004.
  7. Unit distribution.Unit daily medians range from 0.64 to 0.73 EFC; 0003 has the highest single day at 10.80 EFC.
EFC figures use pack-outage-corrected capacity (packs reading near-zero current while siblings are active are excluded from that day's capacity rather than assuming full 6-pack nameplate). Same correction as fleet_findings.ipynb.
